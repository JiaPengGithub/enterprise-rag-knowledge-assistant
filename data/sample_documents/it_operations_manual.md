# IT Operations Manual

## Incident Severity

A severity one incident is any outage that prevents a critical business system from serving employees or customers. Severity one incidents must be escalated to the incident commander immediately and posted in the operations channel within fifteen minutes.

## Access Requests

New access requests must include the system name, business justification, manager approval, and requested duration. Privileged access expires after ninety days unless renewed.

## Backup Verification

Production backup jobs are checked every business day. A restore test is performed once per quarter for each critical system.
