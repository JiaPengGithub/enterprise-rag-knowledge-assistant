# Employee Handbook

## Vacation Requests

Employees should submit planned vacation requests at least ten business days before the requested start date. Managers should approve or reject the request within three business days. Emergency leave can be submitted as soon as practical and does not require the ten-day notice period.

## Expense Reimbursement

Expense reports must include receipts, the business purpose, and the project or department code. Reports should be submitted within thirty days of the expense date.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Teams with customer-facing coverage requirements may define additional scheduling rules.
